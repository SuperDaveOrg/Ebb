Installing

  1. Tap Download preview APK on your phone. Chrome warns that the file might be harmful, as it does for every app from outside the Play Store. Tap Download anyway.
  2. Open the download and tap Install (or Update, if you already have Ebb). The first time, your phone may ask you to allow Chrome to install unknown apps.
  3. If you see App blocked to protect your device, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  4. Open Ebb and allow notifications, so you can check the reminders too.

A preview installs over the released app and keeps its history, and the next release installs over the preview. A preview can have bugs the release doesn't: if you track your own cycle in Ebb, save Back up to a file in Settings before installing.

What to check

About half an hour, at your own pace. You don't need a cycle of your own to help: add a pretend person and make up their history. Ebb keeps everything on the phone, and you can delete it all afterwards in Settings.

1. Switch to your language

Ebb follows the phone's language. To pick one just for Ebb, go to Settings → Language. Check that the first screen and the menus read naturally.

2. Log some history

Log a period starting today, then add a few past ones in History. Read the home screen as it changes: the day of the cycle, and when the next period is expected.

3. The calendar and a day

Tap a day on the calendar. Try flow, the rating, the faces for feelings and a note. In Settings, turn on temperature and cervical mucus, and look at those too.

4. Estimates and charts

Read how Ebb words its estimate and how sure it is. Open Charts. In Settings, turn on the fertile window and moon phases and read what they say.

5. Someone else, and reminders

Add a second person in Settings, so you see the sentences that name someone. Set reminders a day or two ahead of an expected period and read them when they arrive.

6. Settings and help

Read Settings top to bottom, How Ebb works and Your data. Try a backup and a restore, and read every question Ebb asks before it changes something.

The words that matter most

Ebb is careful about what it claims to know. A translation that turns an estimate into a promise, or blurs a period into a cycle, changes what the app says. Please check your language keeps each of these meanings, everywhere it appears.

In EnglishWhat it must mean
periodThe days of bleeding. The plain, everyday word, never a euphemism or slang.
cycleFrom the first day of one period to the first day of the next.
expected / likely betweenAn estimate, never a promise. Ebb gives a range and says how sure it is.
lateThe period hasn't started by when it was expected. A plain fact, never alarming.
fertile window estimateA rough estimate from cycle timing alone. It must never sound reliable enough for contraception.
spottingLight bleeding, logged apart from a period's flow.
cervical mucusThe usual term in your language, as a doctor or a fertility-awareness guide would use it.

Ebb speaks plainly and calmly, in any language. Periods are ordinary, so if a sentence sounds embarrassed, secretive or clinical, that's worth reporting. And Ebb never gives medical advice: if a sentence reads like it's telling someone what their body means, that's a mistake too.
